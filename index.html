Background

Who I am
and what I build

I’m a machine learning engineer with 2.5 years of hands-on experience designing systems that go beyond prototypes into reliable, production-grade deployments. My work is driven by a single question: how do you turn noisy, complex data into decisions that actually matter?

At Freudenberg Xalt Energy Systems, I built ML pipelines that improved anomaly detection in battery systems by up to 25% and cut failure diagnosis time — shipping real value at industrial scale.

At the University of Michigan, my research focused on evaluating how large language models reason over code — studying logical coherence, error identification, and comprehension through rigorous quantitative experimentation.

I’m precise about methodology, obsessive about reproducibility, and driven by the conviction that well-engineered AI is one of the most powerful levers for impact we have.
